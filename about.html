BIOGRAPHY

Loumana's World

Raised between Burkina Faso and Brussels, Loumana sculpts cinematic club music, tours with artists like Blu Samu, Shaka Shams and Roxy Rose, and keeps a monthly radio residency on Bruzz Media while prototyping custom audio tools.

From early keyboard sessions in Ouagadougou to building live shows for Belgium’s most daring rappers, Loumana blends electronic, hip hop and experimental textures into emotive journeys.
