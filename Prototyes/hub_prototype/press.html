Alassane Traoré — known as Loumana — is a producer, DJ, and audio developer based in Brussels. Born in Burkina Faso, he releases on Waffle Street and Cosmic Pop Records, hosts The Waffle Show on BRUZZ, broadcasts from Kiosk Radio, and builds VST plugins and spatial installations.

Stage name from "Louna" — a term of endearment from childhood in Burkina Faso.

Highlights

  • BRUZZ residency — The Waffle Show (4+ years, 98.8 FM Brussels)
  • Kiosk Radio — Parc Royal live video broadcasts
  • Musée du Quai Branly — spatial audio installation (2025–2026)
  • 808 MAX VST — HISE-based bass instrument (in development)
  • Releases: Places EP, Géométrie EP, Nethaan w/ Anthéna, 6 Secondes w/ Mahungu
  • Live: We Can Dance, Fuse, Adorable Collective, Blu Samu touring
