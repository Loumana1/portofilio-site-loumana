Snap To Grid

A rhythm dictionary, built by its community

Snap To Grid is a user-maintained library of rhythms — think WhoSampled, but for grooves, patterns, and beat references instead of samples. Anyone can browse, contribute, and follow how rhythms travel between tracks, genres, and producers. A living archive for people who care about where the pocket comes from.

VST plugin

808 MAX

808 bass for producers who live in the low end

A focused instrument for hip-hop, club, and electronic production — warm sub bass, satisfying glide, and enough grit to cut through a mix without drowning everything else. Built for beatmakers and producers who want their 808 to feel physical in the room, not buried under menus.
