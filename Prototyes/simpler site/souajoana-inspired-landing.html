Snap To Grid

Interactive Rhythm Library & Sequencer Platform

A modern web platform for beat discovery and rhythmic exploration. Features an interactive in-browser step sequencer, multi-track audio playback with real-time stem control, reference track comparisons (e.g. Fred again.. "stayinit"), instant BPM manipulation, and MIDI export.

Musée du Quai Branly

Audio Installation & Sound Archives

An immersive spatial audio installation in collaboration with poet Simon Shungu, exploring archival memory and contemporary sound synthesis. The work repurposes historical sound recordings from the Bibliothèque Nationale de France (BnF) sound archives, weaving multi-channel acoustic spatialization with live spoken-word and deep atmospheric textures.

Simon Shungu (Poetry) BnF Sound Archives Multi-Channel Spatialization Paris, France

808 MAX

Bass & Sub Harmonic Synthesizer

Built from the ground up using HISE scripting and custom analog saturation algorithms, 808 MAX reshapes how producers sculpt low frequencies. Featuring dynamic multi-stage distortion, precise AHDSR envelope shaping, versatile filter curves, and an instant-inspiration workflow.
