WHO IS LOUMANA?

Born in Burkina Faso and raised across Africa before settling in Brussels at 14, Loumana (Alassane Traoré) embodies a unique cultural synthesis. His music is the bridge between West African rhythmic foundations and European electronic urban landscapes.

Starting in Brussels' underground creative spaces, he quickly evolved from a self-taught producer to a key figure in the local scene, notably after winning a pivotal beat battle that caught the attention of Cosmic Pop Records.

HYBRID SETS SPECIALIST

Loumana doesn't just play tracks; he constructs live experiences. Known for "constructing live shows" for artists like Blu Samu, his own sets are a unique fusion of DJing and live performance elements (finger drumming, synths), creating a dynamic energy that bridges the gap between club set and concert.
