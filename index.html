Going on tour

Playing synth, finger drumming to a DJ

As a multidisciplinary artist, I've toured across Europe, performing finger drums and live synthesizer sets. I've been a tour DJ with Antena and OxiHose, playing Moog and VST synths on stage. From intimate clubs to major festivals, I've brought my unique blend of electronic and live instrumentation to venues across the continent.

Radio Residency: BRUZZ

Monthly radio show on Bruzz Media

It's been 4 years since I've been on the radio and I've interviewed a lot of people like Dr. Peet, David Numwami, Blue Samu, and Izzy Kaya. My show is mainly based on the discovery of music. I also host DJs for guest sets and interviews.

Connecting people

Adorable Collective, connecting the genres, connecting people.

Organizing local events in local clubs for me is a way to connect people in the city of Brussels. Through the years, I've organized several events including DJing events, afterworks, Big Club Sporting, TechnoSite, and parties with Afro dancing people. Different crowds, different people - completely different.
