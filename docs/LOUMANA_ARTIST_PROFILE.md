## Bio

Zie Alassane Traoré was born in Burkina Faso in 1998. He grew up in five African countries and moved to Brussels at 14. He makes music as Loumana: composer, beatmaker, DJ, and live player.

The work sits between electronic music, hip-hop, ambient, and orchestral color. The 2019 press text calls the debut style future beat, with Kanye West, Flying Lotus, and Monte Booker named as influences. Later texts add Fela Kuti, Thom Yorke, and Damon Albarn. The Mahungu press text also names Timbaland and Flume, and says beatmaking started at 16. Loumana dates the start to 2011. Use 2011.

He has made music seriously since about 2016 (DJ) and 2017 (the CV’s “producer and sound designer” date). He builds live shows for other artists and hosts a show on BRUZZ.

---

## What he does

- Solo DJ. Sets of 2 to 6 hours. Hip-hop, Afro dance, house, disco, electronic, experimental. Scratching and keyboards. Pioneer DJM-900NXS and CDJ-2000NXS. The performance press kit lists club, rave, festival, and fashion events, at 200 € per hour.
- Tour DJ and live-show builder for other artists, across Europe.
- Producer and beatmaker. Own releases and collaborations.
- Radio host on BRUZZ. The Waffle Show. On air since September 2021. The 2024 bio and the performance press kit say twice a month. The Nethaan press text and a later job CV say monthly.
- Member of Adorable, a Brussels DJ collective started in early 2022. Motto: “Connect genres, connect people.” Members named in the older profile: Loumana, Enfnt, Soria.

## Radio

- **BRUZZ**, The Waffle Show, since September 2021. Interviews, emerging artists, guest mixes. Names attached to the show in the prototype copy: Dr. Peet, David Numwami, Blu Samu, Izzy Kaya. 98.8 FM is what the prototype prints.
- **Kiosk Radio**, Parc Royal, Brussels. Live video sets. The prototype describes club edits, future beats, UK bass, and baile funk. No start year is written down in the sources read here.
